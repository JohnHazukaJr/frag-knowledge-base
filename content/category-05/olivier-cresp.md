# Olivier Cresp

Olivier Cresp is a French perfumer known for creating bold, distinctive fragrances that leave a lasting impression. Coming from a family with deep roots in the fragrance industry, Cresp has crafted some of the most recognizable scents in modern perfumery. He has a talent for building fragrances around a strong central idea, whether that is a sweet [[gourmand]] accord or a warm, spicy composition. His work often blurs the line between designer accessibility and niche creativity, producing scents that are both easy to enjoy and memorable. Cresp's fragrances tend to have excellent longevity and projection, making them favorites for those who want to be noticed.

> He has a talent for building fragrances around a strong central idea, whether that is a sweet gourmand accord or a warm, spicy composition.
