# Jean-Claude Ellena

Jean-Claude Ellena is a perfumer celebrated for his minimalist, refined approach to fragrance. For many years he served as the in-house perfumer for Hermes, where he defined the brand's elegant and understated style. Unlike perfumers who build heavy, complex scents, Ellena is famous for doing more with less, creating transparent compositions that feel light, airy, and effortless. His work often highlights fresh [[citrus]] and clean [[wood|woody]] notes arranged with precision and restraint. Ellena has also written about his craft, offering rare insight into how a master perfumer thinks about scent as a form of art rather than just a product.
