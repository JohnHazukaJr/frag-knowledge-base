# Alberto Morillas

Alberto Morillas is a legendary perfumer with a career spanning several decades and hundreds of fragrances. He is perhaps best known for creating Aqua di Gio by Giorgio Armani, one of the most successful and influential [[aquatic]] fragrances of all time. Morillas has a remarkable range, having worked on everything from fresh, clean designer scents to sweet, comforting [[gourmand]] fragrances. His fresh compositions helped popularize the "blue" aquatic style that dominated men's fragrances for years, while his gourmand work shows a softer, sweeter side. Few perfumers can claim to have shaped the mainstream fragrance world as much as Morillas has.
