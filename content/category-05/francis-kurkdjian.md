# Francis Kurkdjian

Francis Kurkdjian is one of the most celebrated perfumers of the modern era and the co-founder of the niche house Maison Francis Kurkdjian. He rose to fame early in his career and has since worked on countless fragrances for both his own brand and major designer houses. Today he also serves as the perfumer for Dior, giving him influence over some of the most prestigious releases in the industry. Kurkdjian is known for his ability to make fragrances that feel both luxurious and highly wearable, often balancing sweetness and freshness in a way that appeals to a very wide audience. His work with sweet [[gourmand]] and [[floral]] notes has helped define what a modern crowd-pleasing fragrance smells like.
