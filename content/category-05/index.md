# Perfumer

> Behind every great fragrance is a perfumer, often called a "nose." These are the artists who blend raw materials and notes into the finished scents we know and love. A talented perfumer can take the same ingredients everyone has access to and turn them into something completely unique. Many of the most iconic fragrances across designer and niche houses were created by a small group of highly respected perfumers.
