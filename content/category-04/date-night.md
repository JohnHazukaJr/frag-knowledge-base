# Date Night

Date night is where you can bring out your more seductive and attention-grabbing fragrances. These scents are meant to be worn close to someone, so they tend to be warmer, sweeter, and more intimate than what you would wear during the day. Sweet [[gourmand]] notes like vanilla and tonka bean along with warm [[wood|woods]] create a cozy, alluring effect that draws people in. A date night fragrance should be noticeable up close without being suffocating, striking the balance between memorable and comfortable to be around.
