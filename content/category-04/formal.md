# Formal

Formal occasions like weddings, galas, and special events are the perfect time to reach for your most elegant and refined fragrances. These are the scents that feel dressed up and sophisticated, matching the effort you put into your outfit. Rich [[wood|woods]], polished [[floral|florals]], and refined [[gourmand]] notes create a sense of depth and luxury that suits a formal setting. Since these events often take place in the evening or during colder months, you can lean into heavier, longer-lasting compositions that would feel like too much during an average day. A formal fragrance should feel intentional and memorable, leaving a lasting impression.
