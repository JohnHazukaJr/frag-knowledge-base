# Office

Office fragrances need to be professional and considerate of the people around you. In a shared workspace, a scent that is too strong or too sweet can quickly become distracting or even bothersome to coworkers. The best office fragrances are subtle, clean, and applied lightly so they stay within your personal space. Fresh [[citrus]] and [[aquatic]] notes work especially well because they are widely liked and rarely offensive. Save the heavy, bold scents for after work and keep the office rotation understated and polished.
