# Everyday

Everyday fragrances are the scents you can reach for without thinking twice. They are clean, versatile, and non-offensive, which makes them safe to wear to school, running errands, or anywhere you will be around other people. The goal of an everyday fragrance is not to turn heads but to smell fresh and approachable while still being enjoyable. Because they are worn so often, everyday scents usually lean towards fresh [[citrus]] and [[aquatic]] notes that most people find pleasant. Good performance and versatility are the most important qualities here since you want something that works no matter where the day takes you.
