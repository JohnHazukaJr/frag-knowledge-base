# Gym

Gym fragrances are all about staying fresh while you sweat. Since you are moving around and heating up, a scent can project much more than usual, so lighter fresh fragrances are the safest choice in a crowded gym. Clean [[aquatic]] and [[citrus]] notes give an energizing lift without becoming overwhelming when you break a sweat. You generally want to apply less than you normally would and avoid heavy [[gourmand]] or [[wood|woody]] scents that can feel cloying next to body heat and effort. The goal is to smell clean and motivated, not to overpower the person on the treadmill next to you.
