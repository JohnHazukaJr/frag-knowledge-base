# Occasion

> One of the biggest factors in choosing a fragrance is knowing where you plan to wear it. A scent that is perfect for a night out might be overwhelming in the office, and a fresh everyday scent might feel too plain for a formal event. Matching a fragrance to the occasion is one of the easiest ways to make sure you smell great and appropriate for the setting.
