# Floral

Floral fragrances are some of the most popular and recognizable scents in perfumery. They are built around flower-inspired notes that can smell soft, fresh, sweet, creamy, powdery, or even bold depending on the ingredients used. Floral scents are often associated with spring because flowers naturally bloom during that season, but many floral fragrances can be worn all year. A lighter floral fragrance may work well during the day, while deeper floral scents with musk, amber, vanilla, or woods can be better for evenings or colder weather. Depending on the type of floral note being used greatly alters what occasion its used for. This is one of the most diverse note categories as the notes can vary quite a bit.
