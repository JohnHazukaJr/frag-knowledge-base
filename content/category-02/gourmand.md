# Gourmand

Gourmand fragrances are scents that are inspired by foods, desserts, and drinks. They usually have notes such as vanilla, caramel, chocolate, coffee, honey, cinnamon, or tonka bean. They are usually sweeter and heavier than fresh fragrances, which is why they tend to be more popular during the fall and winter. Some gourmand scents can be very sweet, but others use woody, spicy, or floral notes to keep them from smelling too much like candy. Vanilla is probably the most common gourmand note because it works well with so many other notes and can make a fragrance smell warm and smooth. Gourmand fragrances can be comforting, but they can also feel more dressed up depending on the other notes used. Gourmand fragrances tend to lean more feminine than masculine. With that being said vanilla is one of the most mass appealing scents and is used regularly in masculine and feminine scents.
