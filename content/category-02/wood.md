# Wood Notes

Woody notes are used in fragrances to give them a deeper, more natural smell. They can make a scent feel dry, warm, earthy, smoky, or smooth depending on the type of wood used. Some common woody notes are sandalwood, cedarwood, vetiver, patchouli, oakmoss, cashmere wood, and oud. Sandalwood is usually soft and creamy, while cedarwood can smell sharper, drier, and more like freshly cut wood. Vetiver has a grassy and earthy smell that works especially well in fresh fragrances. Oud is usually much stronger and can have a dark, smoky, almost leathery quality. With that being said Oud is not for everyone as some people find it repulsive. Woody notes are often used as base notes because they tend to last longer on the skin than citrus or aquatic notes. They also help hold a fragrance together after the opening fades. Woody fragrances can work in any season, but they are especially nice in colder weather because they usually feel warmer and more noticeable.
