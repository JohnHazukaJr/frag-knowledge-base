# Citrus

One of the most common notes found in fragrances is a the Citrus note.  Commonly found in fresh smelling fragrances citrus gives an energizing effect. Citrus notes are one of the most versatile notes and can be found in most fragrances. One of the downside to citrus notes is that they don't tend to last as long as a lot of other notes so they are typically used as a top note. Citrus scents are most popular to wear in the Spring or Summer but are versatile enough to be used during any season. Citrus can be paired with most other notes but you most commonly find it with aquatic, floral, and oriental notes.

> Citrus notes are often used as top notes because they create a bright, fresh opening.
