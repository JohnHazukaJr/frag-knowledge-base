# Aquatic

Aquatic notes have been in a mainstay in the fragrance game for many years. Typically known as a "blue scent" that most would describe as a blue shower gel smell aquatic fragrances typically lean masculine. Fragrances that use aquatic notes typically tend to be used for an everyday clean smell that is non-offensive to other noses. Aquatic notes work for any event and they work well and effectively. An important thing to note is that most aquatic notes are made from chemicals as it is nearly impossible to derive naturally. Aquatic fragrances usually have beast mode performance and last all day. Since they are typically non-offensive you will find people wearing fragrances that are heavy in this note at, school, the office, date nights, and the gym. Although some may find these notes boring they are sure to pull in more compliments than any other note due to their general likability.
