# Designer 

>Designer fragrances are typically the most common fragrances you’ll find. That is not necessarily a bad thing, but it is mainly because their fragrance houses are larger brands that are more accessible to most consumers. Designer fragrances can range from being incredibly affordable to ridiculously expensive, such as Louis Vuitton. The same can be said about their quality. However, an expensive designer fragrance does not necessarily equal a high-quality fragrance, nor does an affordable designer fragrance mean it is low quality. Designer fragrances are mass appealing and tend to not take big risks which is good for someone who is just getting into fragrances.
