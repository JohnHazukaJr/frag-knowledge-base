# Niche 

>Niche houses are typically the most fun and unique fragrance houses, but that usually comes at a heavy price. These houses tend to use high-quality ingredients while also making their fragrances in smaller batches because they are smaller brands. Due to this, niche houses tend to have more creativity and unique scent profiles. Niche fragrances can be elegant, beautiful scents, or they can smell like literal objects or even be completely heinous. An example of a niche house that specializes in literal smells would be Imaginary Authors. For example, its fragrance The Cobra and the Canary is inspired by asphalt, and it smells exactly like it.
