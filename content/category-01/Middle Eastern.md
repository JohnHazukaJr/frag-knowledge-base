# Middle Eastern 

>Middle Eastern fragrance houses have become increasingly popular around the world, especially in the United States. Fragrance is deeply prominent in many Middle Eastern cultures. Modern Middle Eastern fragrance houses have revolutionized the fragrance industry. While many Middle Eastern houses specialize in creating dupes or hybrids of popular fragrances, there are also brands that create completely original scent profiles that can become huge hits. In addition to all of that, most Middle Eastern fragrances are incredibly affordable, especially in the United States.

## Popular Houses

- *Lataffa*

- *Afnan*

- *Amouage*

- *French Avenue*
