# Summer

> Summer fragrances are fun, fresh, tropical, and fruity typically. Summer fragrances tend to feel like a vacation in a bottle or sunshine. Like winter fragrances they are typically meant for just this season as that's what they are crafted for and can be potentially offensive if used in the polar opposite season. Summer notes lean heavy [[citrus]] as it tends to be the most consistent note.
