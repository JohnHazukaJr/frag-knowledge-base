# Winter

> Winter fragrances are rich, warm, and heavy, crafted to stand up to the cold weather. As temperatures drop, bold scents become far more wearable and even preferable since the cold tends to mute a fragrance's projection. Winter leans heavily into [[wood]], spicy, and sweet [[gourmand]] notes that feel cozy and comforting. Much like summer fragrances, these are usually meant for just this season and can feel overwhelming or even offensive if worn in the heat.

- *Ombre Nomade- Louis Vuitton*
