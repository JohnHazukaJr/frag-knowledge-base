# Fall

> Scents that tend to work well with the fall season are typically [[wood]] and spicy scents. As the weather gets colder like it does in the fall heavier scents become more wearable and even preferable compared to fresh ones that lean more towards warm weather. 

## Popular Fall Fragrances

- *Layton- Parfums de Marly*
- *Jazz Club- Maison Margiela Replica*
- *Angels Share- Killian Paris*
- *Naxos- Xerjoff*
- *Spicebomb- Viktor and Rolf*
- *Tobacco Vanille- Tom Ford*
