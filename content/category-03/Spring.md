# Spring

> Spring is a versatile season that allows people to wear versatile fragrances.  A Precursor to summer a lot of scents with depth should be phased or anything that is too cloying. Similar to [[Summer]] one note profile that is very common during this time is [[citrus]]. Another prominent note during the spring would be [[floral]] and green notes as they invoke fresh garden like smells that we associate with the spring. 

## Popular Spring Scents

- *Parfums de Marly Greenley*

- *Creed Green Irish Tweed*

- *Tom Ford Neroli Portofino*

- *Nishane Wulong Cha X*

- *Issey Miyake L'eau D'issey*
